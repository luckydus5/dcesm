What we do

One firm, the entire build

Most clients arrive with a plot and an intention. We carry it through design, approvals, construction and long term management, so there is never a gap between the drawing and the site.

01

Architectural & structural design

Concept and working drawings with structural calculations, prepared for approval and for the people who will actually build them.

  • Concept and detailed drawings
  • Structural analysis and calculations
  • Approval documentation
02

Building construction

Residential, commercial and institutional buildings, executed by supervised crews against a fixed scope and an agreed programme.

  • Residential and apartment blocks
  • Commercial and office buildings
  • Institutional facilities
03

Civil & infrastructure works

Site works, drainage, retaining structures and access roads. The parts nobody photographs, which decide whether the building lasts.

  • Earthworks and site preparation
  • Storm drainage and retaining walls
  • Access roads and external services
04

Project & cost management

Bills of quantities, tendering, contractor supervision and scheduled cost reporting, so the budget position is never a surprise.

  • Bills of quantities and tendering
  • Contractor supervision
  • Programme and cost reporting
05

Renovation & finishing

Remodelling, extensions and interior fit out for buildings that are tired, unfinished, or no longer suit how they are used.

  • Remodelling and extensions
  • Interior finishing and fit out
  • Structural repairs
06

Estates management

Planned maintenance, tenant and facility coordination and property upkeep for owners who would rather not run it themselves.

  • Planned and reactive maintenance
  • Tenant and facility coordination
  • Property condition reporting

Why clients stay

The difference shows up after handover

Anyone can start a building. The value is in what you find three rainy seasons later. The drainage that still runs. The walls that never cracked. The paperwork that was right the first time.

Discuss your project
  • 01

    One point of accountability

    Design and construction sit under one roof, so there is no gap between the drawing and the site to argue about.

  • 02

    Costed before it is started

    A written bill of quantities up front and reporting against it throughout. Variations are agreed, never discovered.

  • 03

    Qualified supervision on site

    Engineers, not only foremen. Work is checked at each stage against the approved drawings and specification.

  • 04

    We are still here afterwards

How we work

From first call to keys in hand

A predictable sequence, so you always know what stage you are at and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet, visit the site and listen to what you want to end up with, including the budget you have in mind.

  2. 02

    Design & costing

    Drawings, structural design and a written bill of quantities, so you approve a real figure rather than an estimate.

  3. 03

    Approvals

    We prepare and submit the documentation required for permits and follow it through the authorities on your behalf.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Supervised execution against the programme, with site and cost updates issued on schedule rather than on request.

  5. 05

    Handover & care

    Snagging, final inspection and documentation, then optional maintenance and estates management for the long term.
